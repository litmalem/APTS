Authority invariants:
  ELIGIBLE != SELECTED ROUTE
  SELECTED ROUTE = LOGICAL SUBSYSTEM/CAPABILITY IDENTITY ONLY
  SELECTED ROUTE != RUNTIME AVAILABILITY
  SELECTED ROUTE != AUTHORIZATION
  ROUTE SELECTION != DELEGATION
  ROUTE SELECTION != DISPATCH
  ROUTE SELECTION != EXECUTION

Important:
  Stage 3.4.4 ranks/selects only Stage-3.4.3 ELIGIBLE candidates and writes an immutable decision record.
  It does not resolve endpoints, probe health, assign resources, query or grant Governance authorization,
  delegate, dispatch, execute, or actuate.
