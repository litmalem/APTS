8. OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY
-----------------------
Stage 3.7.5 creates a deterministic candidate only. It must not be interpreted as permission to:

  refresh external context;
  query Governance again;
  accept caller-supplied candidate tasks or Plan output;
  create or activate a successor Plan;
  validate a successor Plan;
  commit a replan decision;
  supersede the source Plan;
  transition Mission state;
  route/delegate candidate work;
  dispatch or execute work;
  actuate an external target.

Those capabilities remain outside Stage 3.7.5. The next planned control is Stage 3.7.6.
