Authority invariants:
  CANDIDATE != ELIGIBLE
  ELIGIBLE != SELECTED ROUTE
  ELIGIBILITY != AUTHORIZATION
  COMPATIBILITY != RUNTIME AVAILABILITY
  ELIGIBILITY != DELEGATION
  ELIGIBILITY != EXECUTION

Important:
  Stage 3.4.3 evaluates static canonical eligibility/compatibility only.
  It does not rank/select a route, resolve endpoints, probe health, assign resources,
  query or grant Governance authorization, delegate, dispatch, execute, or actuate.
