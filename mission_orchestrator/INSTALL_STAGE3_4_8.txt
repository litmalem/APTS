Operational boundary
--------------------
Stage 3.4.8 is a control-plane recovery layer. Retry is an admission record, not dispatch. Fallback/reassignment derives only the next immutable Stage-3.4.4 candidate and records a proposal; ownership remains with the Stage-3.4.7 recipient until a later canonical route/Governance/delegation sequence establishes a successor ownership chain.

Conflict boundary
-----------------
Every control chain uses expected_control_count plus transactional sequence guarding. Controls bind their immediate predecessor by ID and SHA-256. FREEZE_OWNERSHIP_CONFLICT is fail-closed and blocks later controls for the ownership receipt.

Governance boundary
-------------------
Stage 3.4.8 performs no new Governance query. The Stage-3.4.5 authorization remains pre-delegation evidence only. Any future dispatch/execution control must perform authoritative pre-dispatch Governance revalidation.
